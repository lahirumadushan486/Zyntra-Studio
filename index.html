    In detail

    Every service, explained.

    01

    Graphic Design

    Creative visual communication designed to make your business look professional, consistent and easy to understand.

    Includes: social posts, Facebook & Instagram ads, posters, flyers, banners, cover photos, carousels, story designs, YouTube thumbnails, business cards, letterheads, quotations & invoices, company profiles, brochures, menus, packaging concepts, print-ready artwork, basic logo & identity support, AI-assisted visuals and background-only versions.

    02

    On-Location Mobile Shooting

    Professional mobile video shoots at your shop, office, event or business location—ideal for social media content, Reels, product promotions and short video advertisements.

    Includes: shop/office/site shoots, product shots, service demos, staff footage, behind-the-scenes, DJI Osmo Mobile 7 or similar gimbal, vertical framing, shot-list prep, editing and upload support. Travel charges may apply depending on location. Shooting prices depend on duration, location and project requirements. Advertising budget is separate.

    03

    Creative Production

    Complete on-location creative production for video advertisements, corporate events, product shoots and professional photoshoots.

    • On-Location Video Ads
    • Corporate Event Coverage
    • Product Shoots
    • Professional Photoshoots
    04

    Video Editing

    We transform raw footage and business ideas into polished videos that are clear, engaging and ready for social media.

    Includes: Reels, TikTok videos, promotional and product videos, business intros, educational and presenter videos, testimonials, short and long-form editing, colour correction, music, voice-over, captions, logo placement, basic motion graphics and multi-platform resizing.

    05

    AI Content Creation

    AI-assisted production helps brands create imaginative visuals and video concepts that may be difficult or expensive to shoot traditionally.

    Includes: AI promotional videos, presenter concepts, cinematic scenes, lifestyle visuals, product concepts, character-consistent scene planning, Sinhala & English voice-over scripts, AI image generation and hybrid editing. Final AI results may vary depending on the source images, generation system and project complexity.

    06

    Social Media Management

    Full page management is charged separately unless specifically included in the selected package.

    Includes: Facebook & Instagram management, scheduling, caption publishing, hashtag placement, basic comment/inbox monitoring, inquiry forwarding, monthly reports and content calendar prep.

    07

    Website Design & Development

    Domain, hosting, backend systems and advanced functionality are quoted separately unless specifically included in the selected website package.

    Includes: business, landing and portfolio websites, responsive layouts, contact forms, WhatsApp & social integration, basic SEO structure and maintenance packages.

    08

    Social Media Content Creation

    Consistent content designed around your business goals, customers and brand identity.

    Includes: monthly planning, content calendars, static & carousel posts, short videos, Reels, Stories, Sinhala/English captions, hashtag research, CTAs, seasonal content and brand-consistent creative.

    09

    Facebook & Instagram Advertising Support

    Advertising service fee is separate. Meta advertising budget is paid separately by the client. Results cannot be guaranteed — final performance depends on the offer, audience, budget, competition and creative quality.

    Includes: Meta Ads Manager setup, WhatsApp/engagement/lead campaigns, audience & location targeting, creative recommendations, ad copy and basic monitoring.

    10

    Content Strategy & Copywriting

    Monthly strategy and campaign planning that gives every post a clear purpose.

    Includes: Sinhala, English and mixed captions, advertising hooks, video & voice-over scripts, CTAs, brand messaging, content series and hashtag sets.

    11

    Print & Business Materials

    Everyday materials that keep your business looking consistent on paper and in person.

    Includes: business cards, letterheads, invoices, quotations, brochures, flyers, posters, banners, signboard concepts and company profiles.
